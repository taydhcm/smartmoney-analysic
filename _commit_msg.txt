## Sprint 10 — Hoàn thành (68/68 tests)

### ml/go_live_checker.py — NEW MODULE
Pre-flight KPI validator trước khi deploy real capital.

**Dataclasses:**
- `GoLiveConfig` — conservative params: p_min=0.70, max_pos=20%, sl_buf=2.0%,
  rr_min=2.0, max_positions=5, min_capital=100M VND
  - `validate() → list[str]` — config validation errors
  - `is_valid() → bool`
- `CheckResult` — name, passed, detail, value, is_warning
- `GoLiveStatus` — checks, all_passed, hard_fails, warnings, score, total
  - `score_pct` property
  - `summary() → str` — GO-LIVE READY / NOT READY
  - `failed_checks()` / `warning_checks()`

**13 Pre-flight checks:**
  1. LightGBM Model (alpha_model.pkl) tồn tại
  2. Batch Calibrator (alpha_calibrator.pkl) tồn tại
  3. E6 Rolling Calibrator (tồn tại + không stale > 7 ngày)
  4. E5 Outcome Tracker (signal_log.db active)
  5. E6 Calibrator Fitted (>= 10 samples)
  6. Drift Alert Check (no active drift từ E5)
  7. Portfolio Capital >= min_capital
  8. P_min >= 0.60 (recommended 0.70)
  9. SL Buffer >= 1.5%
 10. Max Position Size <= 30%
 11. R/R Filter >= 1.5:1
 12. Pipeline Imports (7 modules)
 13. E2 Trade Logger (trades.db sẵn sàng)

**`run_go_live_checks(config, portfolio_capital) → GoLiveStatus`**
- all_passed=True khi không có hard fail
- Warnings không block Go-Live (E6 stale, missing DB files)
